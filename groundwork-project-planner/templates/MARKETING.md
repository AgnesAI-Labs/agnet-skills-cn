# Product Marketing Context

> Shared context for all marketing work. One-off docs (launch plan, competitor data, pricing review, SEO audit) go in `marketing/` later, not here.

## Product Overview

> What it is and what it does, in two or three lines.

## Target Audience

> Who buys or uses it: segment, size, situation.

## Personas

> Two or three named personas: role, goal, frustration, where they hang out.

## Problems & Pain Points

> The problems in order of severity, with how people cope today.

## Competitive Landscape

> Direct competitors, indirect alternatives, and "do nothing".

## Differentiation

> Why choose this over each alternative, one line each.

## Objections

> Reasons people hesitate, each with the honest answer.

## Switching Dynamics

> What pushes people away from their current tool, what pulls them here, what holds them back.

## Customer Language

> Exact phrases users say about the problem and the product. Quote them.

## Brand Voice

> Tone in three words, plus words to always use and never use.

## Proof Points

> Numbers, testimonials, benchmarks, logos. Only real ones.
