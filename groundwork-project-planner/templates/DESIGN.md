# Design

## Vibe

> Three adjectives, one product it should feel like, one it must not look like.

## Colors

> Named tokens with hex values: background, text, primary, accent, error.

## Typography

> Font families, sizes and weights for headings, body and code.

## Components

> Reusable pieces and their rules: buttons, inputs, cards, nav.

## Layout

> Spacing scale, grid, breakpoints, light and dark mode.

## Never

> Design moves that are off-brand, e.g. gradients, emoji in UI, more than two fonts.

## Decisions

> One line per choice or direction change: `- YYYY-MM-DD: what changed - why`.
