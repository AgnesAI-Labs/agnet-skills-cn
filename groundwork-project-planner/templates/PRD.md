# PRD

## Problem

> The pain, in the user's words. One or two lines.

## User

> One specific person or group, and the moment they reach for this.

## Core loop

> The one flow a user repeats, as numbered steps.

## v1 scope

> The minimum that makes it worth using. Bullets.

## Non-goals

> What v1 will not do, even if asked.

## Success

> How you will know it works: a number, a behavior, or a personal test.

## Open questions

> Unknowns that could change scope. Delete when answered.

## Decisions

> One line per choice or direction change: `- YYYY-MM-DD: what changed - why`.
